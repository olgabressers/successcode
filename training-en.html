About the training

I teach Feng Shui and Ba Zi

These are the two foundations of Chinese metaphysics. Ba Zi reads a person's destiny from their date and time of birth. Feng Shui works with the energy of space — your home and your workplace. Together they give a complete picture: who you are, when to act, and how to arrange your surroundings so they support your goals.

What you will learn

Two systems

八字
Ba Zi
Four Pillars of Destiny

A map of personality and destiny from your date and time of birth. You'll learn to read your strengths of character, the favourable and difficult periods of life, the themes of money, career, relationships and health — and to choose an auspicious time for important matters.

風水
Feng Shui
The energy of space

The art of arranging space. You'll learn to see how your home and workplace affect health, finances and relationships, and to tune your surroundings so they work for you, not against you.
